# Changelog

## 1.4.0
- The name of the followed player is shown after the name of a tamed creature (`Wolf -> Ragnar`),
  for every player (can be turned off with `Display / ShowFollowedPlayer`).

## 1.3.1
- First public release.
- Tamed creatures keep their whole body out of buildings and obstacles.
- Roaming destinations need a complete path, enough room to turn around, and no player or pet
  blocking the way.
- Tamed creatures stop instead of pushing players and other pets.
- Random idle time after arriving or being interrupted.
